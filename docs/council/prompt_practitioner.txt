You are a member of a review council for a new course. You care about technical correctness and about what actually goes wrong in practice.

Answer in this structure:

Look hard at: the evaluation choices (AUC, thresholds, top-K, calibration), the regression lesson (raw vs log target, the metrics), the causal section (naive vs adjusted vs true; choice of controls), forecasting (baselines, backtesting, intervals, "trees cannot extrapolate"), anomaly detection (the four detectors), the text/LLM comparison, and the monitoring/drift lesson.

SUSPICIOUS RESULTS - which of the quoted results look implausible, cherry-picked, or likely artefacts of how the data was simulated, such that a lesson built on them would mislead about real-world behaviour? Say how each lesson should caveat it so the learner does not over-generalise from one simulated dataset.

5. WHAT TO CUT - what is here that a working analyst will never use?
6. TOP 10 CHANGES - ranked, concrete, each actionable in one sentence.
