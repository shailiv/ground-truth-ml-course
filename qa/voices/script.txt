Here is why. The test months hold 4,000 advertisers, and 527 of them churned, about one in eight. Accuracy is the share of rows you got right, and every row counts the same. In SQL terms it is an average over a table that is mostly stayers. Call every advertiser a stayer, and the stayers alone hand you 87 percent. The model's edge of under two points is all that its work on churners added. The rarer the outcome, the better doing nothing scores. If only five advertisers in a hundred churned, predicting that nobody churns would be 95 percent accurate, and just as useless.
